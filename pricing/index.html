Questions about pricing

Is ARCA really free? Yes. There's no trial period and no card needed, and every feature in the app is free to use.
Why is it free? We're launching free so that as many people as possible can try ARCA and help decide what we build next.
Will it always be free? If we ever add paid features, we'll tell you in the app first.
Does free mean you sell my data? No. ARCA only holds what you enter and never sells your data. Read about security.

ARCA does not connect to your bank or hold your banking details. It only shows what you enter, and it can never move money. Nothing in ARCA is financial advice.
