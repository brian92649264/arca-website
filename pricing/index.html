Questions about plans

How many bank accounts can I connect? The Free plan lets you connect up to 2 bank accounts. The Basic plan, coming soon, will allow up to 6.
Is the Free plan really free? Yes. There's no trial period and no card needed. You can use the Free plan for as long as you like.
When will Basic be available? We're still building it. You'll see it in the app when it's ready, and nothing changes on the Free plan in the meantime.
Does paying change how my data is used? No. On every plan ARCA has read-only access and never sells your data. Read about security.

ARCA uses TrueLayer to connect to your bank. Account information services are provided by TrueLayer Limited, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 901096). ARCA can only read your data and can never move money.
