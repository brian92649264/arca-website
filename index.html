About ARCA

Built by people living between two countries.

We built ARCA out of our own experience. With bank accounts back home and more where we lived, there was never one place to see the full picture. Every check meant opening several apps and adding everything up by hand.

ARCA is the app we wished we had: one place to add what you spend, wherever you spend it, and see everything together.

Why "ARCA"?

Arca is Latin for a strongbox, the chest Romans used to keep their most valuable things safe. That's the idea behind ARCA: one secure place where your finances come together, so you can see your whole financial picture at a glance.

What you get

Understand your money at a glance.

Add transactions in seconds

A simple form lets you add any transaction in any currency, then edit or delete it whenever you like. No bank login needed.

The full picture

Your balance, income and expenses, all together in one clear view.

Automatic categories

Every transaction sorted into categories like Bills & Housing or Food & Dining. Wrong? Fix it in a tap.

Insights through charts

Spending by category, monthly trends, top merchants, and spending by day and time.

A dashboard that fits you

Add the charts you care about, drag cards into your order, and choose your language, currency and theme.

Stay on budget

Set monthly budgets per category. Progress bars show what's left, and you'll see straight away when one goes over.
