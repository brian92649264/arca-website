About ARCA

Built by people who lived between two countries.

We built ARCA after studying abroad ourselves. With bank accounts back home and more in the country we lived in, there was never one place to see the full picture. Every check meant opening several apps and adding things up by hand.

ARCA is the app we wished we had: connect your accounts once, then see everything together.

What you get

Understand your money at a glance.

Connect your banks

Link accounts from the UK and across the EU, even in different countries. Transactions import automatically, so there's nothing to type in.

The full picture

Balance, income and expenses across every account. Switch between all accounts or a single bank.

Automatic categories

Every transaction sorted into categories like Bills & Housing or Food & Dining. Wrong? Fix it in a tap.

Insights through charts

Spending by category, monthly trends, top merchants, spending by day and time, and how your accounts compare.

A dashboard that fits you

Add the charts you care about, drag cards into your order, and choose your language, currency and theme.

Stay on budget

Set monthly budgets per category. Progress bars show what's left, and you'll see straight away when one goes over.

Who ARCA is for

  • International students and anyone living between two countries
  • People with several bank accounts who want one simple overview
  • Anyone who wants to understand their spending without a spreadsheet

Security first

We can see your transactions. We can never move your money.

Your password stays with your bankYou log in on your own bank's screen, so ARCA never sees or stores your banking password.
Read-only accessARCA can view your transactions, but it can never move your money.
We don't sell your dataYour financial data is used only to show you your own dashboard and insights.
Disconnect any timeRemove an account whenever you like from the Accounts screen.

Regulated bank connection. ARCA connects to your bank through open banking with TrueLayer. Account information services are provided by TrueLayer Limited, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 901096). You can check this on the FCA Register.
