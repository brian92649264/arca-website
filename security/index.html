Security at ARCA

How we keep your financial data safe, in plain English.

  • No bank connection. ARCA doesn't connect to your bank. You add transactions yourself, and ARCA shows them in your dashboard.
  • No banking details. We never ask for your bank login, and ARCA has no way to move money or change anything in your bank account.
  • You're in control. Edit what you've added, or delete your account at any time.

What ARCA can and can't do

ARCA will never ask for your online banking password, in the app, by email or over the phone. If anyone claiming to be ARCA asks for it, don't share it and let us know.

How we protect your data

In transit

  • Everything between the app, our servers, our database and our partners is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

At rest

  • Our database is hosted by Neon in London and is encrypted at rest.
  • Your ARCA password is never stored. We keep only a one-way bcrypt hash.

Your sign-in

  • Sign-in sessions expire automatically, and the long-lived tokens that keep you signed in are stored only in hashed form.
  • Password-reset links expire after 15 minutes and work only once.
  • After several failed sign-in attempts, the account is temporarily locked to stop password guessing.
  • When an account is deleted, any existing sessions for it stop working immediately.

Behind the scenes

  • Each account can only ever access its own data.
  • Secrets such as encryption keys and API keys are kept out of our source code and stored as protected environment variables.
  • Access to our production systems is limited to the ARCA team members who maintain the service, and only when needed to run or support it.

Spending categories and AI

ARCA sorts your transactions into categories such as Food & Dining or Transportation. Incoming money and well-known brands are handled by our own rules on our servers. Anything still unrecognised is categorised by Anthropic's Claude AI model.

  • Only the description you typed is sent to Anthropic. Your name, account details and amounts are never sent, so please don't include personal or sensitive details in a description.
  • Anthropic processes it on our behalf, does not use it to train its models, and deletes it within 30 days (unless it must be kept longer for safety or legal reasons).
  • You can change any category yourself, and we keep your choice.

Bank connections and advice

ARCA doesn't use Open Banking and doesn't access your bank accounts. If we offer bank connections in future, they will be optional, and we will update this page and our Privacy Policy first.

Nothing in ARCA is financial, tax, investment or legal advice.

Found a security issue?

If you think you've found a vulnerability in ARCA, please email smartfinanceapp12@gmail.com with the subject "Security report". Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing it publicly, and don't access or change other people's data while testing. We'll reply as quickly as we can and credit you if you'd like.
