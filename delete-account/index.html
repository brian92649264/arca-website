Delete your ARCA account

How to delete your ARCA account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open the ARCA app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Edit Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app, email smartfinanceapp12@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”. We will confirm once your account has been deleted.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account details, transactions, budgets, categories and connected account dataDeleted from our live database immediately.
Stored bank connection credentialsDeleted immediately; we stop retrieving any data from your bank.
Database backupsKept in our database provider's restore history for up to 7 days, then permanently removed.
Support emailsCopies in our support inbox are kept for up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

You can also disconnect a single bank account at any time on the Accounts page in the app, without deleting your ARCA account. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.

ARCA uses TrueLayer to connect to your bank. Account information services are provided by TrueLayer Limited, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 901096). ARCA can only read your data and can never move money.
